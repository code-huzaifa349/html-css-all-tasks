Fun Facts about Lions

Here are some interesting facts about lions:

  1. Lions are the national animal of several countries, including India and Belgium.
  2. Male lions are known for their distinctive manes, which can be brown, black, or a combination of both.
  3. Lions are social animals and live in groups called prides.
